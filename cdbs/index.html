What the CDBS covers

Real dental care, fully bulk billed*

The Child Dental Benefits Scheme covers the services growing smiles actually need:

Check-ups & cleans

Examinations, scale and cleans, and preventive care to keep little teeth on track.

X-rays & fissure sealants

Digital X-rays where needed, plus sealants that protect adult molars as they come through.

Fillings & extractions

If something needs fixing, treatment is covered too — gently done by dentists great with kids.

Root canals & more

Even bigger treatments are included under the scheme, up to your child’s available balance.

Important information

*For eligible children and teenagers aged 0 to 17 under the Australian Government Child Dental Benefits Scheme (CDBS). Covered services are capped at $1,158 over 2 relevant calendar years. Eligibility criteria apply, including Medicare enrolment and receipt of an eligible payment such as Family Tax Benefit Part A. The scheme excludes orthodontic and cosmetic treatment and services provided in hospital.

Your child’s eligibility and available balance are confirmed with Medicare before any treatment begins, so there are no surprises. Services covered by the CDBS are bulk billed with nothing to pay; any treatment outside the scheme will always be discussed and quoted first.
