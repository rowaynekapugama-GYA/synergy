What happens at your visit · all nine steps, in one appointment

01

A relaxed welcome

We start with a chat, not the chair: your history, any worries, and what you'd love from your smile.

02

Comprehensive examination

Unhurried time with your dentist to check your teeth, gums and bite, and to hear what matters to you.

03

Professional photography

Photos inside and outside your mouth, so you can see exactly what we see on screen.

04

X-rays included*

A look between your teeth and below the gumline, where early problems are easy to miss.

05

Oral cancer check

A quick, gentle screening of your mouth, tongue and throat. Simple, and worth doing.

06

Tooth shade assessment

We record your natural tooth shade, a useful starting point if you ever consider whitening.

07

Scale, clean & polish

Built-up tartar removed and every surface polished. You'll feel the difference walking out.

08

Fluoride treatment

A protective layer applied after your clean to help strengthen your enamel.

09

Personalised treatment plan

Your options explained in plain language, with costs in writing, and time to decide. No pressure.

*One fixed fee of $250 for new patients, normally up to $528 based on our usual fees for the items included. All X-rays required are included, excluding CBCT scans. Any further treatment is always discussed, quoted and agreed with you first. T&Cs apply.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

What's included in The First Visit for new patients?+
All nine steps of a proper first visit in one appointment: a relaxed welcome, comprehensive examination, professional photography inside and outside your mouth, any X-rays required (excluding CBCT), an oral cancer check, tooth shade assessment, scale, clean and polish, fluoride treatment and a personalised treatment plan with costs in writing. One fixed fee of $250 for new patients, normally up to $528. T&Cs apply.
Which health funds do you accept?+
All major health funds are accepted, with on-the-spot claiming through HICAPS so you only pay the gap and know your costs up front. We're also a Child Dental Benefits Schedule (CDBS) provider for eligible children.
Do you offer emergency appointments?+
Yes. For toothache, broken or knocked-out teeth, lost fillings or swelling, call (03) 6361 6721 as soon as possible. We prioritise emergencies and can usually offer same-day or next-day care for the Parkdale and Mordialloc area.
I'm nervous about the dentist. Can you help?+
Absolutely. Our team is experienced with anxious patients: we explain every step, use gentle techniques, never rush you, and offer breaks whenever needed. Many patients tell us their first visit changed how they feel about the dentist.
How do the assessment visits work?+
The Sequence (clear aligners), The Smile Study (veneers and smile design) and The Record (dental implants) are each $295. You leave with photos, scans or imaging as needed, and a written plan with costs, so you can decide in your own time. If you go ahead within 90 days, the $295 is credited in full against your treatment. T&Cs apply.
Where do I park?+
We're at 100 Lower Dandenong Road, Parkdale, a short drive from Mordialloc, Mentone, Aspendale and Cheltenham, with on-site and street parking available for patients.
