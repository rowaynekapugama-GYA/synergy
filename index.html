Good to know

Frequently asked questions

What's included in The First Visit for new patients?+
Everything for a complete first visit: a comprehensive check-up, full mouth clean for tartar removal, bitewing X-rays, an oral cancer check, whitening and smile assessments, intra and extra oral photography, fluoride protection and a personalised treatment plan, valued at $394, for $250. T&Cs apply.
Which health funds do you accept?+
All major health funds are accepted, with on-the-spot claiming through HICAPS so you only pay the gap and know your costs up front. We're also a Child Dental Benefits Schedule (CDBS) provider for eligible children.
Do you offer emergency appointments?+
Yes. For toothache, broken or knocked-out teeth, lost fillings or swelling, call (03) 6361 6721 as soon as possible. We prioritise emergencies and can usually offer same-day or next-day care for the Parkdale and Mordialloc area.
I'm nervous about the dentist. Can you help?+
Absolutely. Our team is experienced with anxious patients: we explain every step, use gentle techniques, never rush you, and offer breaks whenever needed. Many patients tell us their first visit changed how they feel about the dentist.
How do the assessment visits work?+
The Sequence (clear aligners), The Smile Study (veneers and smile design) and The Record (dental implants) are each $295. You leave with photos, scans or imaging as needed, and a written plan with costs, so you can decide in your own time. If you go ahead within 90 days, the $295 is credited in full against your treatment. T&Cs apply.
Where do I park?+
We're at 100 Lower Dandenong Road, Parkdale, a short drive from Mordialloc, Mentone, Aspendale and Cheltenham, with on-site and street parking available for patients.
